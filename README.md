### Hi, I'm Yunus Emre Danabas

**Mechatronics & Electronics Engineering student** at **Sabanci University**, working across **robotics**, **control**, and **embedded systems**.

I graduated **1st in Mechatronics Engineering in 2025** and I'm completing my second degree in **Electronics Engineering, expected 2026**. My work focuses on building robotic systems that connect simulation, sensing, control, and hardware into reproducible end-to-end prototypes.

I have worked and conducted research at **Sabanci University**, **PRISMA Lab** at the University of Naples Federico II, **MIRMI - Technical University of Munich**, **TUBITAK BILGEM**, and **AS Robotics / COMAU Turkey**.

Current interests include **mobile robotics**, **learning-based control**, **human-robot interaction**, **teleoperation**, and **embedded robotic systems**.
